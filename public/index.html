How it works

  1. 1

    Tell it what you know

    A few sentences about your skills and what you have done. Nothing else needed.

  2. 2

    You get three product ideas with the audience, the problem, and a cheap way to test demand before you build.

  3. 3

    Approve the blueprint

    A step-by-step path from your customer's starting point to a clear result.

  4. 4

    Draft the product

    A written guide or a video course with spoken scripts, one section at a time. Plus the sales page text.

  5. 5

    Edit and export

    Download it, print it to PDF, and publish. The draft is yours to improve with real examples.

The writing is a first draft. You add your own real examples and results before selling. It will not invent customers, numbers or reviews.
