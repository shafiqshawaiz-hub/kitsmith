What is Kitsmith?

Kitsmith is a small AI tool that turns what you know into a first draft of a digital product. You describe your skills in a few sentences. It suggests three product ideas, plans one, and writes the draft of a guide or a video course with spoken scripts, plus sales page text. It costs $69 one time for 100 credits.

How Kitsmith works

  1. About you. You type what you know, who you want to help, and any proof you have.
  2. Ideas. It suggests three product ideas, each with an audience, a problem and a cheap way to test demand.
  3. Blueprint. It plans where the customer starts, where they finish and the steps between.
  4. Build. It writes the first draft section by section, as a written guide or a video course with scripts.
  5. Export. You download Markdown or HTML, or print to PDF.

What it costs

$69 one time for 100 credits, sold on Gumroad. A new product (ideas, blueprint and every section once) uses 40 credits, so 100 credits builds 2 products. Each redo of a section, AI edit, rebuilt blueprint or new set of ideas costs 2 credits. If an AI step fails, the credits for that step are returned. Credits do not expire. All sales are final.

What it does not do

It has no unlimited credits, no personal dashboard and no live support. It does not host or sell your product. It cannot promise that a product will sell or that you will earn anything. The output is a first draft: you add your own real examples and check the facts.

What it runs on

Kitsmith uses Anthropic's Claude model. It is told not to invent customers, numbers or reviews, and it marks places where your own material belongs.

Who it is for

People who know something useful and want a structured first draft instead of a blank page, and who are willing to edit it. It is not for anyone who wants a finished product with no work, or who needs support and unlimited use. Compare it with a larger tool on the Synthesise AI alternative page.

Who made it

Kitsmith is made by an independent founder. It is not affiliated with Synthesise AI, Iman Gadzhi, Anthropic or any course. You can find it on Instagram and GitHub.
