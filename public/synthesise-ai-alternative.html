A Synthesise AI alternative for turning what you know into a product

Short answer: Kitsmith is a $69, one-time Synthesise AI alternative that does a similar core job on a smaller scale. You type what you know, it suggests product ideas, plans one, and writes the first draft of a guide or video course. It is not the same tool, and it has less than Synthesise AI lists. What Synthesise AI does

According to its own site, Synthesise AI helps you find where your skills and your audience's problems overlap (it calls this a Unique Value Zone) and builds a step-by-step "product charter". It lists unlimited credits, a personalised dashboard and premium support. A paying user's public tutorial describes the outputs as PDF content and video course outlines with scripts, and says the results need manual polishing and formatting.

The site and the tutorial disagree on how it is sold. The site lists a one-time payment of $2,999. The tutorial says it comes only with Iman Gadzhi's Monetise 2.0 program at $1,995. Check the current terms on their site. See also: what Synthesise AI costs and what the price includes.

What Kitsmith does

  1. Finds the idea. You describe what you know. It suggests three product ideas, each with an audience, a problem, and a cheap way to test demand before you build.
  2. Plans it. A step-by-step path from your customer's starting point to a clear result.
  3. Writes the first draft. A written guide or a video course with spoken scripts, one section at a time.
  4. Writes the sales text. Listing copy you can adapt for a store such as Gumroad.
  5. Exports. Markdown, HTML, or print to PDF.

Kitsmith runs on Anthropic's Claude. It is told not to invent customers, numbers or reviews, and it marks the places where you need to add your own real examples.

Who each one is for

Synthesise AI may suit you if you want unlimited use, a dashboard and support, and you are comfortable with its price and program terms.

Kitsmith may suit you if you want to try the idea-to-first-draft process for $69, you are happy with a fixed number of credits, and you will edit the draft yourself. It does not suit you if you need live support or unlimited generation.

Frequently asked questions

Is Kitsmith the same as Synthesise AI?

No. Kitsmith is a separate, smaller tool that does a similar core job: it finds a product idea from what you know, plans it, and writes a first draft. It does not claim to do the same job or give the same results, and it is not affiliated with Synthesise AI.

How much does Synthesise AI cost?

Its own website lists $2,999 as a one-time payment. A public tutorial says it is only sold with a $1,995 program. Prices change, so check its site.

How much does Kitsmith cost?

$69 one time for 100 credits. One new product uses 40 credits, so 100 credits builds 2 products. Each redo or AI edit costs 2 credits. All sales are final.

What does Kitsmith not have that Synthesise AI lists?

Synthesise AI lists unlimited credits, a personalised dashboard and premium support. Kitsmith has a fixed number of credits, no dashboard and no live support.

Will it make me money?

No tool can promise that. Kitsmith writes a first draft. You add your own real examples, check the facts and test demand before you sell anything.
