How much does Synthesise AI cost?

Short answer: Synthesise AI's own website lists $2,999 as a one-time payment. A public tutorial says it is only sold together with a $1,995 program. The sources disagree, so check the current terms on its site before you decide. Last checked 9 November 2026.

What the Synthesise AI site lists

The pricing section of synthesise.ai shows a single plan, "Starting" at $2,999 as a one-time payment, with these items:

  • Unlimited credits
  • A personalised dashboard
  • Unique Value Zone calculation (it finds where your skills meet your audience's problems)
  • Product charter generation (a step-by-step plan for the product)
  • Premium support and resources

The page does not state a refund policy or a guarantee in the text I could read.

Why sources disagree on the price

A public user tutorial says Synthesise AI is only available with Iman Gadzhi's Monetise 2.0 program at $1,995 and is not sold separately. The tool's own site shows $2,999. Both can be true at different times or for different offers, so treat any number you read, including this one, as something to confirm on the official site.

What you are paying for

At that price you are paying for unlimited use of a tool plus a dashboard and support, and possibly a program around it. If what you need is to try the process of going from "what I know" to "a first draft of a product", you do not need unlimited use to find out whether it works for you.

A cheaper option

Kitsmith is a smaller tool for $69 one time. You get 100 credits. One new product (ideas, plan, and the first draft of every section) costs 40 credits, so 100 credits builds 2 products. Each redo or AI edit costs 2 credits. It does not have unlimited credits, a dashboard or live support, it is not affiliated with Synthesise AI, and all sales are final.
