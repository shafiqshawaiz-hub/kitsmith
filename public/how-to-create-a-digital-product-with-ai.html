How to create a digital product with AI

AI can write a first draft of a guide, template pack or course quickly. It cannot know your experience, test whether anyone wants the product, or make the draft true. This guide covers the order that works and where to be careful. It makes no promises about income.

1. Pick something you know

Write down what you know how to do and what people ask you for. Choose one narrow topic you can explain from experience, such as "fix a dense sourdough loaf" instead of "baking". A narrow topic is easier to write, easier to describe in one sentence, and easier for the right buyer to find. If you are stuck, read digital product ideas from your skills.

2. Test demand before you build

Before you spend hours on a product, make a free one-page version, such as a checklist, and offer it where your audience already spends time. Count how many people ask for more and what they ask. If nobody replies, change the topic or the audience, not the formatting.

3. Plan the result, not the contents

Describe the customer on day one and on the last day. Then list the steps between. A good plan ends in something the customer can do or hold, such as a filled-in sheet, a working template, or one finished task. "Learn about X" is not a result.

4. Draft it with AI, one section at a time

Give the AI your plan and ask for one section at a time. Ask it to mark places where your own example belongs instead of inventing one. Tools such as Kitsmith do this in a fixed order: three ideas with a way to test demand, a blueprint, then a first draft of each section and the sales text. You can do the same with any AI assistant if you keep to the same order.

5. Make it yours

This step is the product. Add your own real examples and results, check every fact and number, and cut anything you cannot stand behind. Never publish invented customers, testimonials or statistics. If a claim needs proof, find it or remove it.

6. Publish and listen

Put it on a store such as Gumroad. Say plainly what the product is and is not, and if it is a first draft, say so. Do not promise results or income. Read the questions people ask and improve the product from them.

Common mistakes

  • Choosing a topic so broad that nobody sees themselves in it.
  • Skipping the demand test and writing for weeks first.
  • Publishing AI text without adding real examples.
  • Making income promises. Many platforms ban them, and regulators treat them as a problem.

Frequently asked questions

Can AI create a whole digital product?

It can draft one. A usable product still needs your real examples, fact-checking and a topic someone wants. Treat the AI output as a first draft.

How long does it take?

A first draft can take minutes. Making it good, with your own examples and a demand test, usually takes days.

Do I have to say it was written with AI?

Some stores and platforms require it. Check the rules of wherever you sell, and be honest with buyers.
