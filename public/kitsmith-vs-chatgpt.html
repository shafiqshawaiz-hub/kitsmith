Kitsmith vs a general AI chat assistant for digital products

A general AI chat assistant such as ChatGPT or Claude can help you write a digital product. Kitsmith is a narrower tool that runs the same job in a fixed order. This page says plainly what the difference is and when you do not need to pay for it. Kitsmith is not affiliated with OpenAI or any chat assistant.

What a general chat assistant can do

You can describe your skills, ask for product ideas, ask for an outline and ask for each section. You control every prompt. If you already know how to prompt well and like working in a chat window, this costs whatever your chat plan costs, and Kitsmith is not necessary.

What Kitsmith adds

  • A fixed order: ideas, blueprint, draft sections, sales text.
  • Prompts written for this job, including an instruction to mark where your own example belongs instead of inventing one.
  • A demand test attached to every idea.
  • Everything stored in one place per product, with one-click export to Markdown, HTML or PDF.
  • A clear credit price: 25 credits per product, 2 per redo.

What Kitsmith does not add

It uses an AI model like a chat assistant does, so it can still be wrong. It has no live support, no dashboard and no unlimited use. The draft still needs your examples and fact-checking. It does not know your business better than you do.

Which should you use

Use a general chat assistant if you are comfortable writing your own prompts and want full control. Try Kitsmith if you would rather follow a set sequence and get an exported draft quickly. Either way, read how to create a digital product with AI, because the steps that matter (test demand, add real examples, check facts) are the same.

Frequently asked questions

Can ChatGPT make a digital product?

It can draft one if you prompt it step by step. You still add real examples and check the facts.

Is Kitsmith better than a general chat assistant?

Not in every case. It saves you from designing the prompts and the order of steps. If you like writing your own prompts, you may not need it.

Does Kitsmith use ChatGPT?

No. Kitsmith runs on Anthropic's Claude model.
